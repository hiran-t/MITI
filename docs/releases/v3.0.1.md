# MITI v3.0.1 — Camera stats in the tab bar, colored log node names

A small follow-up to 3.0 that makes the Camera and Log panels easier to read.

## Camera panel

- **Stats live in the panel's tab bar**, between the tab and the expand/lock buttons, instead of floating over the image: resolution, frames per second, frame size, how far behind the robot the frame is, and the time of the last frame.
- In narrow panels the least useful figures drop out first: the time and frame size, then the resolution. The frame rate and the lag stay visible.
- Other panels can show live status in their tab bar the same way (`PanelStatusPortal`).

## Log panel

- **Each node name has its own color**, so lines from different nodes are easy to tell apart.
  - Colors are handed out in the order nodes first log, so the first 10 nodes never share a color.
  - A node keeps its color while the panel is open, including after Clear.
  - Amber and red are not used for names; they still mean WARN and ERROR.
